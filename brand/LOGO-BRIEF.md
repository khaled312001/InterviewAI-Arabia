# Interprova — Logo design brief & prompts

## The name
**Interprova** = *interview* + *prova* (بروفة — Egyptian Arabic for a rehearsal,
from Italian *prova*). An AI interview trainer for Arabic speakers: you rehearse
the interview before you walk into it.

## Brand colours (locked — taken from the product's design tokens)
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Primary blue | `#2D73FD` |
| Deep blue | `#0736A8` |
| Gold accent | `#FEAF04` |
| Ink / dark ground | `#0B1220` |
| Off-white | `#F5F7FB` |

## The concept: "the rehearsal and the real thing"
Two speech bubbles. The **back** one is an outline (or dashed) — the rehearsal.
The **front** one is solid — the real interview. The offset between them reads as
progress. It also reads as two people in a conversation.

Keeps continuity with the existing speech-bubble mark; adds the new meaning.

## PROMPT 1 — the icon (primary)

Minimalist vector logo mark for "Interprova", an AI interview-rehearsal app.
Two overlapping rounded speech bubbles: the back bubble is an outline only with a
2px stroke in gold #FEAF04, the front bubble is a solid gradient from #2D73FD to
#0736A8, offset down-right so both are clearly readable. Geometric, flat, no
gradients other than the one described, no shadows, no 3D, no bevel. Balanced
inside a square with generous padding. Clean corner radii, consistent stroke
weight, optically centred. Designed to stay legible at 24 pixels. Flat vector,
solid background #F5F7FB. No text, no letters, no words.

## PROMPT 2 — the app icon (Android/iOS)

App icon for "Interprova". Two overlapping speech bubbles — back one an outline
in gold #FEAF04, front one solid white — centred on a deep blue gradient
background from #2D73FD to #0736A8. Bold, simple, high contrast. The mark
occupies the middle 60% of the square so it survives a circular crop. Flat vector,
no text, no letters, no shadows, no borders, no rounded-corner frame.

## PROMPT 3 — the wordmark (lockup)

Horizontal logo lockup for a tech brand named "Interprova". The icon (two
overlapping speech bubbles, gold outline behind, solid blue in front) sits at the
left, the wordmark "Interprova" to its right in a clean geometric sans-serif,
medium weight, tight but comfortable letter spacing, colour #0736A8. The "prova"
half may be a slightly lighter weight than "Inter" to hint at the two words.
Flat vector, white background, generous clear space around the whole lockup.

## Alternate concepts, if the two-bubble route does not land

1. **The clapperboard.** *Prova* also means screen test. A speech bubble whose
   top edge is a film-clapper hinge. Distinctive; risks reading as a video app.
2. **The empty chair.** A minimal chair silhouette facing an implied panel —
   literally the seat you rehearse for. Strong meaning, harder at 24px.
3. **The second take.** A single bubble with a subtle "replay" arc around it.
   Simplest to execute; least distinctive.

## Acceptance checklist (check these BEFORE paying for or shipping any file)

- [ ] Legible at **24×24 px** (browser tab) without losing the two-bubble read
- [ ] Survives a **circular mask** — nothing important outside the middle 66%
- [ ] Works on **#F5F7FB** and on **#0B1220** without a container
- [ ] A **single-colour** version exists (Android 13+ themed icons are alpha-only:
      the silhouette alone must still read)
- [ ] Delivered as **SVG** plus PNG at 512, 192, 96, 48
- [ ] Play Store icon: **512×512, 32-bit PNG, NO transparency, no rounded corners**
      (Play applies the mask itself)
- [ ] Feature graphic: **1024×500**
- [ ] No gold text on white below 16px — `#FEAF04` on white fails contrast for
      text; gold is for shapes and accents only, never small copy
