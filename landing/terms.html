٢2 وصف الخدمةWhat the service is

«ثقتي» أداة تدريب على مقابلات العمل باللغة العربية تعتمد على الذكاء الاصطناعي. تشمل الخدمة:

  • التدريب على الأسئلة: اختيار مجال وظيفي، والإجابة على أسئلة مقابلة، وتلقّي تقييم مكتوب بدرجة من ١٠ على أربعة محاور مع نقاط قوة وضعف وإجابة نموذجية.
  • المقابلة المباشرة: محاورة صوتية ومرئية مع محاوِر ذكاء اصطناعي يسأل ويتابع ويختم بتقييم شامل. متاحة على تطبيق أندرويد وعلى متصفح الويب، مع فروق تقنية تفرضها المنصّة موضّحة داخل التطبيق قبل بدء المقابلة.
  • تحليل السيرة الذاتية: رفع سيرتك الذاتية ووصف الوظيفة المستهدفة ليبني منهما الذكاء الاصطناعي أسئلة مخصّصة.
  • السجل والإحصائيات: عرض جلساتك السابقة ودرجاتك وتطوّرك.

ثقتي ليست منصّة توظيف، ولا وكالة تشغيل، ولا وسيطاً بينك وبين أصحاب الأعمال. نحن لا نتلقّى طلبات توظيف، ولا نُرسل سيرتك الذاتية إلى أي جهة توظيف، ولا نتواصل مع أي صاحب عمل نيابةً عنك.

Thiqty is an Arabic-language, AI-powered job-interview practice tool. The service comprises:

  • Question practice: choose a job category, answer interview questions, and receive written feedback scored out of 10 across four criteria, with strengths, weaknesses and a model answer.
  • Live interview: a voice and video conversation with an AI interviewer that asks, follows up, and closes with an overall evaluation. Available on the Android app and in the web browser, with platform-imposed differences shown inside the app before the interview starts.
  • CV analysis: upload your CV and the target job description so the AI can build questions tailored to you.
  • History and statistics: a view of your past sessions, scores and progress.

Thiqty is not a job board, a recruitment agency, or an intermediary between you and employers. We do not receive job applications, we do not send your CV to any recruiter, and we do not contact any employer on your behalf.

٤4 حدود تقييم الذكاء الاصطناعيThe limits of AI feedback

تقييمنا إرشاد تدريبي — وليس ضماناً للحصول على وظيفة

كل درجة وكل ملاحظة وكل «إجابة نموذجية» تراها في التطبيق هي مخرَج نموذج ذكاء اصطناعي، وليست رأي خبير موارد بشرية ولا تقييماً معتمداً ولا استشارة مهنية أو قانونية.

  • لا نضمن حصولك على وظيفة، ولا اجتيازك مقابلة، ولا دعوتك إلى مقابلة، ولا تحسّن أدائك بأي قدر محدّد. لا يوجد أي التزام بنتيجة من أي نوع.
  • قد يُخطئ الذكاء الاصطناعي. قد يمنحك درجة غير عادلة، أو يقدّم معلومة غير دقيقة، أو يسيء فهم إجابتك، أو ينتج نصّاً غير مناسب للسياق. هذه طبيعة هذه النماذج، لا خللٌ عارض.
  • لا تعتمد على مخرجاتنا كمصدر للحقيقة. راجِع أي معلومة فنّية أو قانونية أو متعلّقة بالراتب من مصدرها الأصلي قبل الاعتماد عليها في مقابلة حقيقية.
  • الأسئلة تدريبية. لا نزعم أنها الأسئلة التي ستُطرح عليك فعلاً في مقابلة بعينها، ولا أن لدينا صلة بأي صاحب عمل.

أنت المسؤول عن قراراتك المهنية. استخدم التطبيق كما تستخدم شريكاً تتمرّن معه: مفيد للتمرين، لا يُلزمك رأيه.

7.2 Minute balance

After the trial you buy a balance of minutes to spend on interviews. Consumption is measured by the actual interview time, as metered on our servers — not by a count of questions. Purchased minutes never expire, are not reset at the end of a day or a month, and you may buy more at any time; balances accumulate. The minute packs and their prices are shown in the app and on the pricing page.

7.3 The monthly subscription

A subscription gives you a monthly allowance of minutes at a better per-minute rate, plus subscriber-only features listed on the subscription screen in the app. One distinction matters before you buy:

Purchased balance vs. subscription allowance
  • Minutes bought in a pack never expire and stay in your account whether or not you subscribe.
  • A subscription allowance is temporary and ends with the billing cycle; it does not roll over. When your subscription ends, any remaining allowance lapses, while your purchased balance is untouched.
  • When an interview starts, the subscription allowance is spent first, then your permanent balance — so the balance that never expires is preserved.

Prices are quoted in Egyptian pounds and include any applicable taxes unless stated otherwise, and are shown to you clearly before payment is completed.

Material points about payment — read before buying
  • A subscription is a one-off payment for a fixed period, and it does not auto-renew. We operate no recurring billing and take no repeat charge from your card. When the paid period ends, your account reverts to the free tier automatically and nothing is charged unless you buy again.
  • Purchases — whether a minute pack or a subscription — are made through the web version of the service, with payment processed by the Egyptian gateway EasyKash (card, an e-wallet such as Vodafone Cash or InstaPay, or Fawry). The Android app sells nothing inside itself; it reads your balance and subscription status and unlocks what corresponds to them.

Some limits remain, imposed by the platform rather than by our policy: speech recognition on Android depends on the recognition service installed on your device, and session recording may be restricted to what your device's operating system permits. 8.2 Refunds

In accordance with Egyptian Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018, you may request a refund of what you bought within fourteen days of purchase, provided you have not made substantial use of it during that period.

"Substantial use" here is a number, not a judgement call: for a minute pack it means having consumed more than 10% of the pack's minutes. Because every credit and debit is recorded in your ledger, you can check exactly what was consumed from inside the app before you ask. For a subscription it means having consumed more than 10% of your cycle allowance, or having made repeated use of subscriber-only features.

  • Send your refund request to info@barmagly.tech from your registered email address, stating the purchase date and transaction reference.
  • We respond within seven working days. Once approved, the refund is issued through the payment gateway to the original payment method within fourteen working days of approval; your bank or wallet provider may take additional time to show the amount.
  • When a refund is issued, the corresponding minutes are withdrawn from your balance and the refunded subscription is cancelled immediately; this is recorded in your balance ledger exactly as any credit is.
  • If a refund reaches us after the minutes have been spent — whether we approved it or your bank imposed it as a payment dispute — we recover only what is actually left of the balance. We never drive it negative, so a later purchase of yours is never quietly consumed paying off an earlier one that was refunded. The difference — minutes consumed whose price was returned — remains a debt owed to us, and we may suspend the account until it is settled. Both amounts appear in your balance ledger, so you and we can see exactly what happened.
